## ✨ What is StreamNest?

StreamNest was built around a simple idea:

> **Why jump between different platforms when your entertainment can live in one place?**

The platform combines multiple forms of digital entertainment into a single interface while maintaining dedicated experiences for each type of content.

From watching a live channel to listening to a radio station or playing a podcast episode, StreamNest provides a unified way to discover and consume content.

# 🚀 Features

### 📺 Live Streaming

Browse available streaming channels and launch playback directly from the platform.

### 📻 Live Radio

Stream radio stations with station artwork, metadata, playback controls, favorites, and listening history.

### 🎙️ Podcast Platform

StreamNest includes a complete podcast experience:

- Podcast discovery
- Category-based discovery
- RSS-powered podcast synchronization
- Podcast detail pages
- Episode pages
- Episode artwork
- Episode metadata
- Audio playback
- Show notes
- Playback history

### 🔎 Content Discovery

Explore entertainment through categories and search across the platform.

### ❤️ Favorites

Save content to your personal Library for quick access later.

### 🕘 Playback History

StreamNest keeps track of recently played content across supported media types.

### 📚 Unified Library

The Library provides a central place for saved and recently played content.
